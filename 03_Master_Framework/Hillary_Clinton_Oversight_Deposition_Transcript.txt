DEPOSITION OF FORMER SECRETARY OF STATE HILLARY RODHAM CLINTON
BEFORE THE HOUSE COMMITTEE ON OVERSIGHT AND GOVERNMENT REFORM
IN THE MATTER OF THE INVESTIGATION INTO JEFFREY EPSTEIN

Location: Chappaqua Performing Arts Center, Chappaqua, New York
Duration: Approximately 4 hours, 35 minutes
Format: Closed-door deposition

Ranking Member: Rep. Robert Garcia (D-CA)

Republican Members Present: Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC), Rep. Lauren Boebert (R-CO), Rep. Scott Perry (R-PA), and others

OPENING STATEMENT — SECRETARY HILLARY CLINTON:

SECRETARY CLINTON: "As I stated in my sworn declaration on January 13, I had no idea about their criminal activities. I do not recall ever encountering Mr. Epstein. I never flew on his plane or visited his island, homes or offices. I have nothing to add to that. Like every decent person, I have been horrified by what we have learned about their crimes. It's unfathomable that Mr. Epstein initially got a slap on the wrist in 2008, which allowed him to continue his predatory practices for another decade."

[Source: Newsweek, March 2, 2026; CBS News, February 26, 2026]

SECRETARY CLINTON [from prepared opening statement]: [The committee's actions have been] "designed to protect one political party and one public official, rather than to seek truth and justice for the victims and survivors."

[Source: CBS News, February 26, 2026]

SECRETARY CLINTON [from prepared opening statement]: [The committee compelled her testimony] "fully aware that I have no knowledge that would assist your investigation, in order to distract attention from President Trump's actions and cover them up despite legitimate calls for answers."

[Source: CBS News, February 26, 2026]

---

[UNVERIFIED GAP — Early questioning by committee members]

QUESTIONING — GENERAL QUESTIONS ON EPSTEIN:

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I knew nothing about him."

[Source: CBS News, March 2, 2026 — in reference to Jeffrey Epstein]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [In preparation for the testimony she was made aware that Epstein had attended an event at the White House but she did not recall ever meeting Epstein.]

[Source: CBS News, March 2, 2026]

INCIDENT — LEAKED PHOTOGRAPH (approximately 1 hour 20 minutes into deposition):

[Clinton's lawyer interjected questioning to notify that Rep. Lauren Boebert (R-CO) had shared a photograph taken during the closed-door deposition with right-wing commentator Benny Johnson, who posted it on social media — a violation of the agreed-upon ground rules for the deposition.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I'm done with this! If you guys are doing that, I am done. You can hold me in contempt from now until the cows come home. This is just typical behavior."

[Secretary Clinton stood up from her seat.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I'm done for now."

SECRETARY CLINTON: "Oh, for heaven's sakes."

[Source: CNN, March 2, 2026; CBS News, March 2, 2026]

[The deposition was paused while the two sides negotiated how to move forward. Rep. Boebert eventually said she would take the photo down.]

SECRETARY CLINTON [after resumption]: "I will confess that I had some concerns about whether the majority on the committee would treat me fairly and ... fairly convey what I say and what I did and how I looked and how I responded."

SECRETARY CLINTON: [If the hearing was public, the photo would not have been an issue.]

[Source: CNN, March 2, 2026]

EXCHANGE — REP. NANCY MACE (R-SC) ON PHOTOGRAPHS OF BILL CLINTON:

[Rep. Mace repeatedly asked Hillary Clinton to share her reactions to photos showing Bill Clinton with women and Epstein.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Declined to weigh in on the photos and repeatedly said she would not share such "opinions" and that she was there to testify about specific matters.]

REP. MACE: [Persisted with questions.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Suggested Mace was employing "innuendo."]

REP. MACE: [Said she was asking "black-and-white" questions.]

REP. MACE: [Asked whether Clinton did anything to investigate her husband's interactions with Epstein after learning about the allegations against him.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I don't find any of your questions to be relevant to the investigation."

[Source: CNN, March 2, 2026]

---

[UNVERIFIED GAP]

---

EXCHANGE — REP. NANCY MACE (R-SC) ON HOWARD LUTNICK:

REP. MACE: [Asked how Hillary Clinton knew Howard Lutnick in the early 2000s.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Began answering, recalling that Lutnick led financial services firm Cantor Fitzgerald, which lost hundreds of employees in the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks. At the time, Clinton was a New York senator.]

REP. MACE: [Interrupted.]

[An unidentified voice instructed Mace to let Clinton finish.]

REP. MACE: "I know that."

SECRETARY CLINTON: "You asked the question, I'm going to answer your question. This was what I spent my time doing."

[Source: CBS News, March 2, 2026; CNN, March 2, 2026]

REP. MACE: [Said there is an email from Lutnick to Epstein about an "intimate event" for Hillary Clinton at Cantor Fitzgerald and accused Clinton of trying to "obfuscate" by denying that she ever tried to get money from Epstein.]

REP. MACE: "You want to yell at me, that's fine, but I'll yell right back."

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Knew nothing about the email or event.]

[Source: CBS News, March 2, 2026]

REP. MACE: [Accused Clinton of being "defiant and indignant" in her responses.]

REP. MACE: [Asserted that she was] "a survivor trying to look out for other survivors" [and that the panel had waited six months to hear Clinton's testimony.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Banged the table as she responded:] "I was taking care of the people who lost 3,000 lives at the World Trade Center."

[Source: CNN, March 2, 2026]

---

[UNVERIFIED GAP]

EXCHANGE — REP. LAUREN BOEBERT (R-CO) ON "PIZZAGATE":

REP. BOEBERT: [Asked whether Hillary Clinton had reviewed any emails in the Epstein files pertaining to the "Pizzagate" conspiracy theory from the 2016 election season.]

CLINTON'S LAWYER: [Referred to it as "wacky" while trying to clarify the question.]

REP. BOEBERT: "You can characterize it however you want."

[Clinton's other lawyer clarified the question again. The former secretary of state gave a wry smile.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: "Pizzagate was totally made up. It was an outrageous allegation that ended up hurting a number of people that caused a deranged young man to show up with his assault rifle and shoot up a local pizzeria. I can't believe you're even referencing it."

REP. BOEBERT: "There are connections to the — the language."

SECRETARY CLINTON: "Well, the QAnon people believe there are."

[Lengthy exchange continued.]

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I expected a lot of interesting questions today, but Pizzagate was not on my list."

[Source: CNN, March 2, 2026]

POST-DEPOSITION — SECRETARY CLINTON TO REPORTERS:

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Told reporters she answered] "every one of their questions as fully as I could based on what I knew."

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I never met Jeffrey Epstein. Never had any connection or communication with him. I knew Ghislaine Maxwell casually as an acquaintance."

[Source: CBS News, February 26, 2026]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Maxwell attended her daughter's wedding as a guest of someone who was invited.]

[Source: CBS News, February 26, 2026]

SECRETARY CLINTON: "I'm not going to do it again."

SECRETARY CLINTON: "They had a chance to do it in public, and I wish they had done it in public."

[Source: CBS News, February 26, 2026]

SECRETARY CLINTON: [Republicans also asked her about UFOs and the Pizzagate conspiracy theory. At the end, the deposition got] "quite unusual."

[Source: CBS News, February 26, 2026]
