## Section 4: Schedule P/C Deadline Context (March 8, 2026)

### Overview

All events in this update occur in the final 10 days before Schedule Policy/Career (Schedule P/C) rules take effect, which structurally transforms the federal workforce.

### Verified Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Legal basis | Executive Order 14171 (Jan 20, 2025); revival of 2020 Schedule F concept |
| Final rule published | February 5-6, 2026 (Federal Register) |
| Effective date | **March 8, 2026** (30 days after publication) |
| Positions affected | ~50,000 career federal employees in "policy-influencing" roles |
| Classification change | Career employees converted to at-will status |
| Removed protections | MSPB appeal rights; Chapter 43/75 Title 5 due process protections |
| Retained protections | Whistleblower retaliation protections (enforcement shifted to agencies); anti-discrimination |
| Public comment opposition | 94%+ of public comments opposed the rule |

### Structural Implications

| Before March 8 | After March 8 |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Career federal employees have civil service protections | ~50,000 positions converted to at-will |
| Adverse actions require due process | Removal without traditional civil service procedures |
| MSPB appeal rights | Appeals limited to district court (discrimination/whistleblower claims only) |
| External oversight (Office of Special Counsel) | Enforcement shifted internally to agencies |
