### Why This Is a Confrontation Node

The Anatoly Kolodkin's arrival creates a multi-layered escalation trigger:

1. **Legal framework already in place**: Treasury amended sanctions waiver on March 20 to specifically exclude Cuba. Russian oil delivery to Cuba is now explicitly prohibited under U.S. sanctions.
2. **The vessel is already sanctioned**: By the U.S., EU, and UK for its role in Russian oil trade post-Ukraine invasion.
3. **Precedent set**: The Skipper seizure (December 10, 2025) established that the U.S. will physically interdict oil tankers. The DOJ forfeiture complaint revealed 1.1 million barrels of the Skipper's cargo were destined for Cubametales — Cuba's state oil importer. This was already Cuba's oil being seized.
4. **Military assets in position**: SOUTHCOM is already tracking the vessel. The same naval infrastructure used for Operation Southern Spear (Venezuela blockade) is operational in the Caribbean.
5. **Timing**: Updated tracking data places arrival ~March 23, 2026 (earlier than initial ~April 4 estimate). If delivered successfully, the confrontation itself and its aftermath may still resonate into the April 12–20 convergence window. If intercepted, the fallout would dominate news cycles for days.

## Embassy Standoff — Diplomatic Implications

The diesel refusal creates a cascading diplomatic situation:

- **Cuba's position**: "The Ministry interprets as shameless the claim by the diplomatic mission to access a good as a privilege that it denies to the Cuban people." Logically coherent from their perspective — the U.S. blockaded their fuel, and now wants fuel for its own embassy.
- **U.S. Embassy reserves**: Enough diesel for approximately one more month. If not resupplied, nonessential staff may need to evacuate by May.
- **Reciprocal reduction**: Staff reduction at Havana embassy would trigger U.S. demand for similar reduction at Cuban Embassy in Washington — further severing diplomatic channels during active crisis.
- **Spanish Embassy blocked**: Spain's embassy has excess fuel from imported diesel for hotels (now closed). Cuba denied permission for Spain to redistribute to other European embassies. Cuba is controlling fuel distribution as leverage — the same tool being used against them.
- **Talks confirmed**: Díaz-Canel confirmed March 14 that Cuba has held talks with the U.S. government — first confirmation of direct discussions amid the pressure campaign.
