## Silicon Sovereignty Thesis

**Core Hypothesis**: Whoever controls data + compute + algorithms has significant influence over information flows at scale.

**The Convergence**:
```
Data Layer (Gaming + Social Media)
    ↓
Compute Layer (Stargate + Oracle Cloud)
    ↓
Algorithm Layer (AI training + content moderation)
    ↓
Unified Control
```

**Key Observation**: Same entities appear across all three layers.

## Arkansas Regulatory Loop

### Acts 373 and 548 (2025)

**Act 373** (Generating Arkansas Jobs Act):
- Creates Strategic Investment Rider mechanism
- Iterative resubmission: PSC denial is procedurally temporary
- No provision for final denial
- Signed March 20, 2025 with emergency clause

**Act 548**:
- Creates two-tier data center tax exemption
- "Nonadjacent" aggregation clause enables combining separate sites
- Allows statewide aggregation into single tax-exempt entity
