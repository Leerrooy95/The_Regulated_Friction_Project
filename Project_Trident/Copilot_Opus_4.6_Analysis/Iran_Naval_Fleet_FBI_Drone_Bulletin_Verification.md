## Event 2: FBI California Drone Threat Bulletin (March 11, 2026)

**Context**: On March 11, 2026, ABC News reported on an FBI bulletin distributed to California law enforcement agencies in late February 2026.

**Key details from the bulletin**:
> "We recently acquired information that as of early February 2026, Iran allegedly aspired to conduct a surprise attack using unmanned aerial vehicles from an unidentified vessel off the coast of the United States Homeland, specifically against unspecified targets in California, in the event that the US conducted strikes against Iran. We have no additional information on the timing, method, target, or perpetrators of this alleged attack."

**FBI caveat**: Intelligence described as "uncorroborated" and "cautionary" — no evidence Iran has current capability or active plans to execute such an operation from a vessel in the Pacific.

**Timeline**:
- **Early February 2026**: FBI acquires the intelligence
- **Late February 2026**: Warning distributed to California law enforcement
- **February 28, 2026**: US-Israel launch strikes on Iran (the contingency the bulletin referenced)
- **March 9, 2026**: Trump declares Iran has "no navy"
- **March 11, 2026**: Bulletin publicly reported by ABC News

**Response**: Gov. Newsom confirmed awareness; California emergency operations center activated when war began. FBI declined comment.

**Sources**: [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/fbi-warns-iran-aspired-drone-attack-california-11662062), [Fox LA](https://www.foxla.com/news/fbi-warning-iran-drone-threat-california), [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/iran-drone-attack-west-coast-warning-b2936690.html), [Newsmax](https://www.newsmax.com/politics/fbi-drones-california/2026/03/11/id/1249159/), [Yahoo/AP](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/fbi-warns-iran-aspired-attack-173459997.html)
