# Project 2025 (the "2025 Presidential Transition Project"): A Factual Overview

## TL;DR
- **Project 2025, formally the "2025 Presidential Transition Project," is a Heritage Foundation–led coalition effort** organized/launched in April 2022 and publicly unveiled with its policy book on April 21, 2023; it is built on four pillars (a policy agenda/Mandate for Leadership, a personnel database, a training academy, and a 180-day playbook), and its flagship document is the ~900-page "Mandate for Leadership: The Conservative Promise" (30 chapters across 5 sections).
- **As of September 2026 the official site, project2025.org, still resolves but has been reduced to a stripped-down static Heritage "Mandate for Leadership" landing page** (past-tense wording); the project's policy-drafting operations formally wound down after the 2024 party conventions (director Paul Dans resigned July 30, 2024), while Heritage continues related work and many contributors now serve in the second Trump administration.
- **The organizers describe it as a governing agenda to prepare a conservative administration; mainstream reporting and critics describe it as a blueprint to consolidate executive power, replace civil servants with loyalists, and enact a hard-right social agenda** — and the Center for Progressive Reform/Governing for Impact tracker reports 53% of its domestic administrative policy agenda (283 of 532 tracked actions) initiated or completed within Trump's first year.

## Key Findings

**What it is / who / when.** Project 2025 is a political initiative organized by The Heritage Foundation [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_2025) together with a coalition that grew to more than 110 conservative organizations. Heritage announced the project and hired Paul Dans as director in April 2022; the flagship policy book was published April 21, 2023. [heritage](https://www.heritage.org/press/project-2025-publishes-comprehensive-policy-guide-mandate-leadership-the-conservative-promise) Kevin Roberts is Heritage's president. Wikidata lists the project's inception as April 14, 2022.

**Four pillars.** (1) A policy agenda — the "Mandate for Leadership: The Conservative Promise" book; (2) Personnel — an online "Presidential Personnel Database" (nicknamed the "conservative LinkedIn"); (3) Training — the "Presidential Administration Academy," on-demand training for prospective appointees; (4) A 180-day "Playbook" of agency-by-agency transition actions [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_contributors_to_Project_2025) for the first 180 days after inauguration.

**The book.** ~900 pages (variously cited as 887 or ~920), edited by Paul Dans and Steven Groves, with more than 35 primary authors and — per Heritage/coalition materials — "more than 400 scholars and policy experts." It is the ninth edition [Heritage Foundation](https://www.heritage.org/press/project-2025-publishes-comprehensive-policy-guide-mandate-leadership-the-conservative-promise) in Heritage's "Mandate for Leadership" series dating to 1981. It contains 30 chapters organized into five sections, plus a foreword and afterword.

**Stated goals (organizers' words).** Project 2025's site states: "It is not enough for conservatives to win elections. If we are going to rescue the country from the grip of the radical Left, we need both a governing agenda and the right people in place, ready to carry this agenda out on day one of the next conservative administration."

**Current status (Sept 2026).** project2025.org still resolves but now serves a single static "Mandate for Leadership" marketing page; policy operations wound down after the 2024 conventions; Paul Dans resigned July 30, 2024. Heritage transitioned rather than shut down the project. Many Project 2025 authors now serve in the Trump administration (e.g., Russ Vought at OMB). Independent trackers report roughly half the domestic agenda initiated/completed in year one.

## Details

### 1. Basic factual description
Project 2025 — officially the "2025 Presidential Transition Project" — is a coalition initiative spearheaded by The Heritage Foundation, a conservative think tank [The Wild Hunt](https://wildhunt.org/2026/01/project-2025s-architect-updates-its-vision-for-america.html) founded in 1973. According to Heritage's own materials, the project was launched in April 2022; the book's foreword states the volume is "the opening salvo of the 2025 Presidential Transition Project, launched by The Heritage Foundation and our many partners in April 2022." The policy book was formally published April 21, 2023. Some references (e.g., the Wikipedia infobox) list "April 21, 2023" as the establishment/publication date because that is when the Mandate was released; Wikidata lists inception as April 14, 2022. Donald Trump praised the forthcoming project at a Heritage event in Amelia Island, Florida, on April 21, 2022.

The coalition grew from roughly 45 organizations (2022) to more than 100, and Heritage President Kevin Roberts stated on July 30, 2024, that the project had brought together "over 110 leading conservative organizations." Advisory-board "coalition partners" include groups such as the Alliance Defending Freedom, Moms for Liberty, Turning Point USA, Susan B. Anthony Pro-Life America, and America First Legal.

**The four pillars** (from Heritage/project materials):
1. **Policy agenda** — the "Mandate for Leadership: The Conservative Promise" book.
2. **Personnel** — an online Presidential Personnel Database ("conservative LinkedIn"). ProPublica reported in August 2024 that "under Dans, the project has assembled a database of more than 10,000 names — job candidates vetted for loyalty to Trump's cause — who will be ready to deploy into federal agencies should he win the 2024 election."
3. **Training** — the Presidential Administration Academy, "on-demand training sessions" [The Heritage Foundation](https://www.heritage.org/conservatism/commentary/project-2025) to turn "future conservative political appointees into experts in governmental effectiveness." [The Heritage Foundation](https://www.heritage.org/conservatism/commentary/project-2025)
4. **Playbook** — a 180-day transition playbook of agency-by-agency actions to begin January 20.

Leadership: Paul Dans (director, until August 2024), Spencer Chretien (associate director), Steven Groves (co-editor), Kevin Roberts (Heritage president).

### 2. Stated goals (in the project's own words)
From the project's website / manifesto: [Michigan Advance](https://michiganadvance.com/2024/01/16/project-2025-if-allowed-will-cement-america-as-a-rightwing-authoritarian-state/) "The actions of liberal politicians in Washington have created a desperate need and unique opportunity for conservatives to start undoing the damage the Left has wrought and build a better country for all Americans in 2025. It is not enough for conservatives to win elections. If we are going to rescue the country from the grip of the radical Left, we need both a governing agenda and the right people in place, ready to carry this agenda out on day one of the next conservative administration. This is the goal of the 2025 Presidential Transition Project." [Data For Progress](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2023/8/24/voters-broadly-reject-key-climate-components-of-the-conservative-project-2025)

The book's stated goal (Paul Dans's foreword): "Our goal is to assemble an army of aligned, vetted, trained, and prepared conservatives to go to work on Day One to deconstruct the Administrative State." [Heritage](https://static.heritage.org/project2025/2025_MandateForLeadership_FULL.pdf) Kevin Roberts (Heritage) framed it as ensuring "the next president has the right policy and personnel necessary to dismantle the administrative state and restore self-governance to the American people." [heritage](https://www.heritage.org/press/project-2025-publishes-comprehensive-policy-guide-mandate-leadership-the-conservative-promise) The book identifies four broad "fronts": (1) restore the family as the centerpiece of American life; [Heritage](https://static.heritage.org/project2025/2025_MandateForLeadership_FULL.pdf) (2) dismantle the administrative state; (3) defend the nation's sovereignty, borders, and bounty against global threats; (4) secure God-given individual rights to live freely.

### 4. Current status (as of September 2026) & 2025–2026 developments
- **Director resignation (July 30, 2024):** [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/30/politics/project-2025-paul-dans/) Paul Dans resigned as director; Kevin Roberts said the project "completed exactly what it set out to do: bringing together over 110 leading conservative organizations to create a unified conservative vision." The move followed sustained criticism, including from Trump himself (who called some proposals [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/voices/columns/ajish-p-joy/2024/08/01/project-2025-head-paul-dans-resigns-after-trump-criticism.html) "ridiculous and abysmal," and whose co-campaign managers said "reports of Project 2025's demise would be greatly welcomed"). Reporting (CNN, ProPublica, Semafor) indicated [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2024/07/30/politics/project-2025-paul-dans/) policy-drafting operations were winding down as planned after the party conventions, while personnel work would continue. It was not a full shutdown.
- **Book postponement:** Kevin Roberts's own book "Dawn's Early Light" (foreword by JD Vance) was postponed from Sept. 24, 2024, to after the election amid the backlash.
- **Website status:** As of September 2026, project2025.org still resolves but has been reduced to a static Heritage "Mandate for Leadership" landing page written in past tense ("This was a product of more than 100 organizations to prepare for a new conservative administration through policy, training, and personnel"), with a "Buy the Book" link to shop.heritage.org; interior pages (e.g., /about/, /about/advisory-board/) now collapse to that landing page. Subdomains apply.project2025.org and training.project2025.org reportedly still exist. No Cloudflare bot-challenge was observed on fetch, but automated access to the live official site may be unreliable.
- **Implementation relative to the Trump administration:** After winning the 2024 election, Trump nominated multiple Project 2025 authors/contributors to his administration — most prominently [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/trump-project-2025-russell-vought-office-management-budget-rcna181453) Russ Vought (author of the Executive Office of the President chapter) as OMB director, plus [Factually](https://factually.co/fact-checks/politics/project-2025-authors-appointments-in-current-administration-5795eb) Brendan Carr (FCC chair), John Ratcliffe (CIA), Peter Navarro (trade), Tom Homan (border), and others. CNN found "at least 140 people who worked in the Trump administration had a hand in Project 2025... including more than half of the people listed as authors, editors and contributors to 'Mandate for Leadership'" (and nearly 240 people overall with ties to both). Independent trackers report substantial implementation: the Center for Progressive Reform / Governing for Impact tracker reported "The Trump administration has initiated or completed 53 percent of Project 2025's domestic administrative policy agenda in the 12 months following the president's inauguration. In all, 283 of the 532 recommended actions identified in this tracker have been put into action" (February 2026 update; the prior October 15, 2025 update was 251 of 532, just over 47%). A separate crowd-run tracker (project2025.observer) put completion near 48–50%.
- **"Project 2026" / Heritage 2.0:** In early 2025 Heritage released [Substack](https://antiauthoritarianplaybook.substack.com/p/what-is-project-2026-478) "Restoring America's Promise: 2025–2026 Policy Priorities" (nine priorities dubbed "Heritage 2.0"), described by some outlets (NOTUS) as a successor agenda; [Substack](https://antiauthoritarianplaybook.substack.com/p/what-is-project-2026-478) Heritage says there is no formal "Project 2026." In January 2026 Heritage published a family-policy report, [The Wild Hunt](https://wildhunt.org/2026/01/project-2025s-architect-updates-its-vision-for-america.html) "Saving America by Saving the Family: A Foundation for the Next 250 Years."
- **Lawsuits/controversies:** Project 2025 itself is a policy document, not a defendant; no major lawsuit against Project 2025 as an entity was identified. However, many second-term Trump actions aligned with it have drawn litigation (e.g., DOGE data-aggregation/Privacy Act lawsuits, DEI executive-order challenges). Journalist David A. Graham (The Atlantic) published a New York Times–bestselling book, "The Project: How Project 2025 Is Reshaping America" [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/the-project-explores-project-2025s-origins-and-goals-to-reshape-american-culture) (2025).

### 6. Range of characterization (neutral documentation)
**Organizers' framing:** Heritage describes Project 2025 as "the conservative movement's unified effort to be ready for the next conservative administration to govern at 12:00 noon, January 20, 2025," [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/project-2025-plan-remake-america-explained-1235056542/) and as separate from any campaign: "As we've been saying for more than two years now, Project 2025 does not speak for any candidate or campaign." Roberts frames the goal as to "dismantle the administrative state and restore self-governance to the American people."

**Mainstream reporting:** Outlets such as AP, CBS News, and CNN describe it as [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/what-is-project-2025-trump-conservative-blueprint-heritage-foundation/) "a detailed blueprint for the next Republican president to usher in a sweeping overhaul of the executive branch," led by Heritage and staffed largely by former Trump officials — "essentially a government-in-waiting for the former president's second term" (Lisa Mascaro, AP). CNN, Axios, and NBC News documented extensive overlap between Project 2025 contributors and Trump's orbit despite Trump's campaign-trail disavowals.

**Critics:** Democratic officials and advocacy groups (e.g., the ACLU, the Brennan Center, and Rep. Jared Huffman's "Stop Project 2025 Task Force") characterize it as an authoritarian plan to "gut checks and balances," concentrate power in the presidency via unitary-executive theory, replace career civil servants with loyalists, and enact a hard-right social agenda (restricting abortion, ending DEI, dismantling the Department of Education). The ACLU calls it "a roadmap for how to replace the rule of law with right-wing ideals." [ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/project-2025-explained)

## Recommendations
- **Anchor primary-source claims to:** the Heritage press release of April 21, 2023 (chapter list + publication facts), the full PDF on static.heritage.org (foreword, pillars, mission language), and the project's own "about" mission text (quoted above). Because the live site is now gutted, use the Wayback advisory-board snapshot for the original coalition/mission wording.
- **Guard against link rot:** Prefer the static.heritage.org PDF and the Internet Archive mirrors over the live project2025.org site, which is now a static landing page and may not reflect original content. Capture a fresh Wayback snapshot of any live page you cite.
- **Attribute implementation figures precisely:** Use the Center for Progressive Reform/Governing for Impact tracker for the 53% figure (283 of 532 actions, February 2026 update) and project2025.observer for the ~48% figure, and note these are independent/advocacy trackers, not Heritage figures.
- **Benchmarks that would change the picture:** If Heritage relaunches a full project2025.org site or formally announces a "Project 2026," update the status section; if a court ruling directly names Project 2025 or blocks a signature recommendation, add it to the timeline; if a tracker's methodology or completion figure shifts materially, refresh the implementation section.

## Caveats
- **Date nuance:** Sources disagree on the "launch" date. Heritage's book says the project launched April 2022; the book/publication date is April 21, 2023; Wikidata cites inception April 14, 2022; Wikipedia's infobox lists April 21, 2023. Best practice: describe it as organized/launched in April 2022 with the book published April 21, 2023.
- **Page count varies** (887, ~900, 920) depending on whether front/back matter is counted; "~900 pages" is the safest phrasing.
- **Author counts vary** (35+ primary authors; ~40 primary authors per Wikipedia; "more than 400 scholars and policy experts" total per Heritage/coalition materials) — cite as "more than 35 primary authors and more than 400 total contributors."
- **Website status** could not be verified to the HTTP-status level; the description reflects content returned as of the research date plus secondary reporting. Confirm the live site before publishing.
- **Implementation trackers** are run by advocacy organizations or independent volunteers, not neutral government bodies; figures are estimates and methodology-dependent.
- **Trump–Project 2025 relationship is contested:** Trump publicly disavowed the project during the 2024 campaign ("I have no idea who is behind it"), while critics and reporting emphasize the heavy personnel overlap and post-election appointments of its authors.
