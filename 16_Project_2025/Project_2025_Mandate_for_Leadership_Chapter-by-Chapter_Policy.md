# Project 2025 "Mandate for Leadership": Chapter-by-Chapter Policy Directive Summary

## TL;DR
- This report summarizes the specific policy directives in all 30 chapters of Project 2025's *Mandate for Leadership: The Conservative Promise* — the 922‑page, ninth‑edition volume released by The Heritage Foundation on April 21, 2023, written by more than 35 primary authors and edited by Paul Dans and Steven Groves — organized in the book's five sections.
- Recurring cross-chapter themes include eliminating or restructuring agencies (Education, DHS, CFPB, Export‑Import Bank), replacing career civil servants with loyal political appointees via a revived Schedule F, and rolling back DEI, climate, and abortion-related programs.
- Each chapter summary below names concrete actions; source URLs are provided per chapter for citation.

## Key Findings
The book's central organizing ideas are consolidating executive power under the president (unitary executive theory), dismantling the "administrative state," and reorienting agencies away from DEI, climate, and abortion policies. The most consequential structural proposals are the outright elimination of the Department of Education, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, and the Export-Import Bank; the break-up of the Department of Homeland Security and NOAA; and the government-wide reclassification of career employees to make them easier to remove. (Under the related Schedule F concept — renamed "Schedule Policy/Career" when enacted by rule in 2025 — the Office of Personnel Management estimated roughly 50,000 positions, about 2% of the federal workforce, would be reclassified into at‑will status.)

## Details

### Section 1 — Taking the Reins of Government

**1. White House Office (Rick Dearborn).** Recommends structuring every White House office (Chief of Staff, Counsel, Presidential Personnel, Communications, etc.) around loyalty to the president's agenda, with the Office of Presidential Personnel conducting "political background checks" and handling firings; [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) suggests reexamining media workspace on the White House campus since "no legal entitlement exists" for permanent press space. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**2. Executive Office of the President (Russ Vought).** Calls for using OMB's statutory and procedural levers to bring the bureaucracy in line with the president's will; eliminating National Security Council directorates not aligned with the president; and abolishing the Gender Policy Council and the interagency Social Cost of Carbon Working Group. Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**3. Central Personnel Agencies (Donald Devine, Dennis Dean Kirk, Paul Dans).** Recommends reviving Schedule F to reclassify large numbers of career civil servants as at-will employees who can be removed, giving the president authority to remove holdover political appointees and career staff, imposing more rigorous performance evaluations, and reforming federal retirement benefits. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

### Section 2 — The Common Defense

**4. Department of Defense (Christopher Miller).** Recommends abolishing DEI offices, ending "Marxist indoctrination" and CRT programs, reversing policies allowing transgender individuals to serve (and expelling those with gender dysphoria), reinstating service members discharged for refusing the COVID vaccine with back pay, and providing DOD support to DHS border operations. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**5. Department of Homeland Security (Ken Cuccinelli).** Primary recommendation is to pursue legislation to dismantle DHS, combining CBP, ICE, USCIS and immigration-court functions into a standalone Cabinet-level border/immigration agency; privatizing the TSA; moving other components (Coast Guard, Secret Service, FEMA, CISA) to other departments; and repealing Temporary Protected Status designations, ending the Flores settlement, and creating Title 42-style expulsion authority. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**6. Department of State (Kiron Skinner).** Recommends that all non-career leadership resign by noon January 20 and be replaced with political appointees loyal to the president (without Senate confirmation where possible), freezing unratified treaties pending review, requiring ambassadors to submit resignation letters, enforcing strict visa reciprocity, and taking a more confrontational posture toward China. Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**7. Intelligence Community (Dustin Carmack).** Recommends restructuring the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, removing "underperforming" employees, replacing IC "woke culture" and identity politics with "traditional American values," revising Executive Order 12333 to ensure the DNI is aligned with the president, and making the CIA more responsive to presidential priorities. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**8. Media Agencies — USAGM & CPB (Mora Namdar; Mike Gonzalez).** For the U.S. Agency for Global Media, recommends reform and, if reform fails, defunding and disestablishment, plus moving Voice of America oversight closer to the NSC; [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) for the Corporation for Public Broadcasting, recommends the president defund CPB entirely by refusing to sign any appropriations bill funding it and stripping NPR/PBS of their noncommercial educational status. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**9. Agency for International Development (Max Primorac).** Recommends "deradicalizing" USAID by ending all climate-change programs and withdrawing from Paris Agreement-related work, dismantling the DEI apparatus (eliminating the Chief Diversity Officer and DEI clauses in contracts), ending promotion of abortion and "gender radicalism," installing pro-life political appointees, and reorienting the agency to counter China. [The Stennett Report](https://thestennettreport.github.io/Project_2025/Mandate_For_Leadership/Chapter_9.html) Source: https://thefulcrum.us/governance-legislation/project-2025-usaid

### Section 3 — The General Welfare

**10. Department of Agriculture (Daren Bakst).** Recommends the USDA stop prioritizing climate/environmental issues over food production, withdraw from U.N. sustainable-development efforts, reduce/reform/eliminate farm subsidies, reimpose SNAP work requirements and reform categorical eligibility, eliminate the Community Eligibility Provision for free school meals, and repeal the federal GMO labeling mandate. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf

**11. Department of Education (Lindsey Burke).** Recommends eliminating the Department of Education outright; block-granting the roughly $16–18 billion Title I program to states and phasing it out over 10 years (after which "states should assume decision-making control"); converting the roughly $13 billion IDEA special-education program to block grants or a private school-choice offering; privatizing the federal student loan portfolio (moving it out of the department), ending Public Service Loan Forgiveness, and consolidating income-driven repayment plans; expanding school choice; and rewriting Title IX to remove gender-identity protections. Source: https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/project-2025-would-dramatically-cut-federal-funds-for-schools-then-what/2024/07

**12. Department of Energy and Related Commissions (Bernard McNamee).** Recommends eliminating or reforming the Loan Programs Office, ARPA-E, the Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations, and the Grid Deployment Office; [Energy Central](https://www.energycentral.com/energy-biz/post/project-2025-what-it-says-about-energy-environment-and-public-lands-Qxcq0Fe7uUSreNa) eliminating energy-efficiency standards for appliances; [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/4769252-project-2025-climate-change-energy-environment/) ending "climate-change interference" in LNG export approvals; and refocusing FERC as an economic (not climate) regulator. [Energy Central](https://www.energycentral.com/energy-biz/post/project-2025-what-it-says-about-energy-environment-and-public-lands-Qxcq0Fe7uUSreNa) Source: https://envirodatagov.org/project-2025-department-of-energy-and-related-commissions-annotated/

**13. Environmental Protection Agency (Mandy Gunasekara).** Recommends updating (effectively rescinding) the 2009 greenhouse-gas endangerment finding, shifting more environmental authority to states, eliminating "duplicative" programs and offices, restricting which scientific studies EPA may use in rulemaking, and drafting a Day One executive order to enact these reforms. [wearebridge](https://wearebridge.com/documents/BRIDGE_Project_2025_Primer.pdf) Source: https://envirodatagov.org/project2025-environmental-protection-agency-annotated/

**14. Department of Health and Human Services (Roger Severino).** Recommends the FDA reverse its approval of the abortion drug mifepristone [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/project-2025-plan-remake-america-explained-1235056542/) and enforce restrictions on mailing abortion pills; ending funding of research using fetal tissue; making Medicare Advantage the default enrollment option; [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/sph/news/articles/2024/project-2025-could-become-a-political-reality-that-would-upend-medical-practice/) adding Medicaid work requirements and caps; and reversing Section 1557 nondiscrimination protections based on gender identity. Sources: https://healthlaw.org/authoritarian-project-2025-agenda-endangers-the-future-of-medicaid-and-the-affordable-care-act/ ; https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-features/maga-plan-attack-birth-control-surveil-women-ban-abortion-pill-1234934807/

**15. Department of Housing and Urban Development (Ben Carson).** Recommends devolving most HUD functions to states/localities and consolidating remaining functions elsewhere, [Shelterforce](https://shelterforce.org/2024/09/26/how-project-2025-would-dismantle-hud/) ending the "Affirmatively Furthering Fair Housing" rule, [Civil Rights](https://civilrights.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Project-2025-Fair-Housing-Lending.pdf) ending use of "disparate impact" enforcement, ending racial-bias appraisal reforms, and prioritizing single-family homeownership. Source: https://shelterforce.org/2024/09/26/how-project-2025-would-dismantle-hud/

**16. Department of the Interior (William Perry Pendley).** Recommends reinstating oil and gas leasing in the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge [Center for Western Priorities](https://westernpriorities.org/2026/01/from-disavowal-to-delivery-the-trump-administrations-rapid-implementation-of-project-2025-on-public-lands/) and opening the National Petroleum Reserve-Alaska, revoking Biden's "30x30"/America the Beautiful conservation plan, [MeatEater](https://www.themeateater.com/conservation/public-lands-and-waters/how-project-2025-could-impact-public-lands) scaling back Endangered Species Act and Migratory Bird Treaty Act protections, downsizing national monuments and repealing/curtailing the Antiquities Act, [MeatEater](https://www.themeateater.com/conservation/public-lands-and-waters/how-project-2025-could-impact-public-lands) and expediting drilling permits. Source: https://westernpriorities.org/2024/07/project-2025-would-devastate-americas-public-lands/

**17. Department of Justice (Gene Hamilton).** Recommends expanding political appointees throughout DOJ, placing the FBI under a politically accountable appointee and reviewing major FBI investigations, [Civil Rights](https://civilrights.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Project-2025-Justice-Reform.pdf) aligning litigation strategy with the president's agenda, [Law Dork](https://www.lawdork.com/p/project-2025-justice-department-plans) curtailing/restructuring the Civil Rights Division, [Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/analysis/unmasking-the-anti-democracy-agenda-of-project-2025/) vigorously enforcing the Comstock Act against mailing abortion pills, [Brennan Center for Justice](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/analysis-opinion/project-2025s-plan-criminal-justice-under-trump) and prioritizing immigration enforcement including against sanctuary jurisdictions. [Law Dork](https://www.lawdork.com/p/project-2025-justice-department-plans) Source: https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/analysis-opinion/project-2025s-plan-criminal-justice-under-trump

**18. Department of Labor and Related Agencies (Jonathan Berry).** Recommends allowing comp-time in lieu of overtime pay (Working Families Flexibility Act), setting regional overtime thresholds, [Center for American Progress](https://www.americanprogress.org/article/project-2025-would-cut-access-to-overtime-pay/) mandating a "Sabbath" day of rest, [The Fulcrum](https://thefulcrum.us/governance-legislation/project-2025-department-of-labor) reorienting the EEOC toward religious/disability/pregnancy accommodation claims, expanding religious exemptions for contractors, rolling back union protections, and eliminating DEI programs. Source: https://thefulcrum.us/governance-legislation/project-2025-department-of-labor

**19. Department of Transportation (Diana Furchtgott-Roth).** Recommends resetting NHTSA fuel-economy standards to lower levels feasible for internal-combustion vehicles (rolling back EV-forcing standards), [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/project-2025-pushes-automated-trucks-pumps-brakes-on-evs) reducing transit funding and worker protections, restructuring/potentially privatizing the FAA's air traffic functions, and reviewing the Jones Act. [senate](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/project2025_transportation.pdf) Source: https://www.freightwaves.com/news/project-2025-pushes-automated-trucks-pumps-brakes-on-evs

**20. Department of Veterans Affairs (Brooks Tucker).** Recommends increasing privatization/ [Task \& Purpose](https://taskandpurpose.com/military-life/project-2025-for-military-veterans/) community care, revising the VA disability rating schedule to reduce future awards, [Indyapwu](https://indyapwu.org/VHPI-Project-2025.pdf) rescinding VA abortion and gender-reassignment surgery policies, [Military Times](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2024/07/24/how-would-project-2025-impact-troops-and-veterans/) increasing patients-per-provider, [Substack](https://dougporter.substack.com/p/project-2025s-assembly-line-veteran) reviewing remote work, and replacing career staff with aligned appointees. Source: https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2024/07/24/how-would-project-2025-impact-troops-and-veterans/

### Section 4 — The Economy

**21. Department of Commerce (Thomas Gilman).** Recommends dismantling/breaking up NOAA [Substack](https://ellieleonard.substack.com/p/project-2025-21-the-department-of) and commercializing the National Weather Service's forecasting, [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/4907338-heritage-foundation-plan-weather-service/) abolishing the Economic Development Administration, elevating the Office of Space Commerce, adding a citizenship question to the census, and tightening technology export controls. [Project 2025 Tracker](https://www.project2025.observer/en?agencies=Dept.+of+Commerce) Source: https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/4907338-heritage-foundation-plan-weather-service/

**22. Department of the Treasury (William Walton, Stephen Moore, David Burton).** Recommends replacing the seven individual income-tax brackets with two rates of 15% and 30% (the 30% rate starting near the Social Security wage base, ~$168,600) while eliminating most deductions and credits, cutting the corporate rate to 18% (from 21%), taxing capital gains at 15%, rescinding the IRA's IRS funding increase, and closing the IRS Office of Equity, Diversity and Inclusion. [Tax Notes](https://www.taxnotes.com/featured-analysis/your-guide-tax-policy-project-2025/2024/07/03/7kfp7) Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/project-2025-tax-trump-economy-heritage-foundation-how-it-works/

**23. Export-Import Bank (Veronique de Rugy; opposing view by Jennifer Hazelton).** The lead essay recommends abolishing the Export-Import Bank, arguing it "plays a marginal role in export financing—backing a mere 2 percent of US exports each year" and that the CBO found its programs would cost taxpayers billions; a companion essay by Hazelton argues for retaining it. Source: https://bryanalexander.org/book-club/reading-project-2025-part-8-redoing-the-economy-and-what-it-means-for-higher-education/

**24. Federal Reserve (Paul Winfree).** Recommends eliminating the Fed's "dual mandate" so it focuses on price stability alone (removing full employment), [Whatdoesproject2025say](https://whatdoesproject2025say.com/contextual-analysis/section-4-4-federal-reserve/) limiting the Fed's asset purchases and lender-of-last-resort role, [Uwsp](https://blog.uwsp.edu/cps/2024/09/12/the-project-2025-monetary-policy-gold-standard-and-federal-reserve/) and considering more radical alternatives up to abolishing the Fed in favor of "free banking" or a return to a commodity/gold standard. [The Stennett Report](https://thestennettreport.github.io/Project_2025/Mandate_For_Leadership/Chapter_24.html) Source: https://thefulcrum.us/governance-legislation/project-2025-federal-reserve

**25. Small Business Administration (Karen Kerrigan).** Recommends aggressively reviewing COVID-era loans for fraud, ending the SBA's targeting of "politically favored" or DEI-based businesses, reducing SBA disaster lending in favor of the private insurance market, [Bryan Alexander](https://bryanalexander.org/book-club/reading-project-2025-part-8-redoing-the-economy-and-what-it-means-for-higher-education/) and general governance reforms. Source: https://bryanalexander.org/book-club/reading-project-2025-part-8-redoing-the-economy-and-what-it-means-for-higher-education/

**26. Trade (Peter Navarro "fair trade"; Kent Lassman "free trade").** Presents two opposing conservative visions: Navarro argues for tariffs and economic decoupling from China, urging enactment of the U.S. Reciprocal Trade Act to let the president match other countries' tariff rates; Lassman argues for lowering or repealing tariffs and pursuing free trade. Source: https://www.piie.com/blogs/realtime-economics/2024/trumps-economic-agenda-could-disrupt-us-and-world-economies

### Section 5 — Independent Regulatory Agencies

**27. Financial Regulatory Agencies — SEC & CFPB (David Burton; Robert Bowes).** Recommends abolishing the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and returning its functions to banking regulators and the FTC, [CLP Blog](https://clpblog.citizen.org/how-would-the-cfpb-fare-under-heritages-project-2025/) repealing Dodd-Frank Section 1071 small-business data collection, [W3woody](https://w3woody.com/2024/07/09/claude-summarizes-project-2025-chapter-27-so-you-dont-have-to/) and scaling back SEC climate-disclosure rules and other regulations. Source: https://clpblog.citizen.org/how-would-the-cfpb-fare-under-heritages-project-2025/

**28. Federal Communications Commission (Brendan Carr).** Recommends "reining in Big Tech" [The First Amendment Encyclopedia](https://firstamendment.mtsu.edu/article/brendan-carr/) by reinterpreting Section 230 to limit content-moderation immunity, requiring transparency on algorithms and moderation, [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-fcc-brendan-carr-project-2025-what-to-know/) banning TikTok as a national security threat, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/18/trumps-fcc-pick-has-said-he-wants-to-ban-tiktok-challenge-section-230) expanding the FCC's "covered list" of Chinese equipment, [Truth on the Market](https://truthonthemarket.com/2024/11/21/what-project-2025-can-tell-us-about-brendan-carrs-fcc-priorities/) and having Big Tech contribute to the Universal Service Fund. Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-fcc-brendan-carr-project-2025-what-to-know/

**29. Federal Election Commission (Hans von Spakovsky).** Recommends limiting FEC enforcement against those not clearly violating the law and raising campaign contribution limits, [harvard](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/2022/feed_items/12422225) generally reducing federal regulatory oversight of election-related activity. Source: https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/2022/feed_items/12422225

**30. Federal Trade Commission (Adam Candeub).** Recommends generally reducing antitrust enforcement [harvard](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/2022/feed_items/12422225) while opening the door to a "broader" antitrust view targeting Big Tech's effects on free speech and democracy, using the FTC against corporate ESG/DEI practices, and pursuing social-media firms over harms to minors. [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/02/03/2025/big-tech-critic-adam-candeub-fcc) Source: https://thefulcrum.us/governance-legislation/project-2025-federal-trade-commission

## Recommendations
- For citation, pair the primary PDF (https://static.heritage.org/project2025/2025_MandateForLeadership_FULL.pdf) with the per-chapter secondary source listed above; the primary text is authoritative for exact wording and page numbers.
- Where exact quotes or page numbers matter, verify against the primary PDF, since several secondary sources are advocacy/analysis sites.
- Treat the split chapters (23 Export-Import Bank; 26 Trade) as presenting two opposing views rather than a single unified recommendation.

## Caveats
- Several analysis sources cited are advocacy organizations; their framing may be editorial even where the underlying factual proposal they describe is accurate. Quotes and specific proposals were cross-checked against the primary text and multiple outlets.
- Distinguish the 2023 blueprint's *proposals* from what has since been *enacted* — for example, Medicaid work requirements were enacted through 2025 legislation, and the EPA's endangerment-finding rescission and Schedule F–style reclassification were pursued through separate 2025 rulemaking, distinct from the document's recommendations.
- Chapter numbering for combined sub-parts (Ch. 8 media agencies, Ch. 23 Export-Import Bank, Ch. 26 trade, Ch. 27 SEC/CFPB) reflects the book's own structure, in which two authors contribute paired or opposing sections under a single chapter.
